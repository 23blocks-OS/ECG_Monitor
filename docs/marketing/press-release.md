## Open-Source ECG Monitoring System Launches Free License for Medical Institutions, Non-Profits, and Government Healthcare

*Comprehensive cardiac monitoring platform eliminates $50,000+ cost barrier for community clinics and underserved healthcare facilities*

**[City, State] – November 6, 2025** – A new open-source ECG monitoring system is now available free of charge to medical institutions, non-profit healthcare organizations, and government facilities, potentially saving healthcare organizations tens of thousands of dollars while improving access to cardiac care in underserved communities.

The ECG Monitor system, developed by software engineer and Ironman triathlete Juan Pelaez following his own near-fatal heart attack at age 49, provides professional-grade 3-lead ECG monitoring, AI-powered arrhythmia detection, and comprehensive patient management capabilities – at a fraction of the cost of traditional medical ECG systems.

### Breaking Down Cost Barriers

Traditional medical-grade ECG systems cost $50,000 to $100,000+, putting them out of reach for many community clinics, rural health centers, and non-profit healthcare providers. The ECG Monitor system reduces that barrier to approximately $30-50 per month in cloud infrastructure costs, plus one-time hardware costs of $115 per monitoring device.

"After my heart attack, I realized that financial barriers prevent many clinics from offering quality cardiac monitoring," said Juan Pelaez, creator of the ECG Monitor system. "This project is about making enterprise-level heart monitoring accessible to every community, regardless of budget."

### Dual Portal System for Comprehensive Care

The system features two complementary web portals:

**Provider Portal** – Enables healthcare professionals to:
- Monitor multiple patients from a single dashboard
- Search and select patients within their organization
- View real-time ECG data for any patient
- Track organization-wide statistics
- Manage shared device pools

**Patient Portal** – Empowers patients with:
- Personal health dashboards
- Access to own ECG recordings and metrics
- AI-powered health insights
- Historical data tracking

### AI-Powered Clinical Support

The system integrates Anthropic's Claude AI to analyze ECG metrics and detect:
- Atrial fibrillation (AFib)
- Premature ventricular contractions (PVCs)
- Heart rate variability patterns
- Exercise vs. rest differentiation
- Anomaly patterns requiring clinical attention

### Free License, Open Source

Under a new dual-use license, the software is completely free for:
- Medical institutions (hospitals, clinics, medical practices)
- Non-profit healthcare organizations
- Government healthcare facilities
- Research institutions (with IRB approval)
- Personal health monitoring

"We're seeing growing interest from community health centers, rural clinics, and faith-based health ministries," Pelaez noted. "Organizations that couldn't previously afford ECG monitoring can now deploy a complete system in about two hours."

### Technical Architecture

Built on modern cloud infrastructure, the system includes:
- Raspberry Pi-based ECG data collection (using ADS1293 3-channel ECG module)
- AWS cloud processing (IoT Core, Lambda, DynamoDB)
- Real-time data streaming and analysis
- HIPAA-ready security controls
- Role-based access (admin, doctor, nurse, patient)

### Cost Comparison

**Traditional Medical ECG System:**
- Initial cost: $50,000-$100,000+
- Annual maintenance: $5,000-$10,000
- Vendor lock-in

**ECG Monitor (Open Source):**
- Software license: $0 (free for medical/non-profit)
- Monthly AWS: $30-50 for small clinic
- Hardware per device: $115 (one-time)
- First year total: ~$1,000-1,500
- No vendor lock-in

### About the Creator

Juan Pelaez is a software engineer and 10-time Ironman finisher who survived a major heart attack in August 2024 at age 49 during training for his 11th Ironman. Despite being in peak physical condition, a 95% blockage in his left anterior descending artery ("the widowmaker") led to his cardiac event. After 100% recovery (rare for such severe blockages), he completed Ironman Arizona in November 2024 – exactly one year after his planned race was derailed by his heart attack. The ECG Monitor system was born from this experience and his belief that quality cardiac monitoring should be accessible to all.

### Technical Specifications

- **Sampling Rate**: 250 Hz
- **ECG Leads**: 3-lead configuration (I, II, III)
- **AI Model**: Claude 3.5 Sonnet (Anthropic)
- **Cloud Platform**: AWS (Lambda, DynamoDB, IoT Core, S3)
- **Hardware**: Raspberry Pi 4, CJMCU-1293 (ADS1293)
- **Security**: Encryption at rest and in transit, HIPAA-ready controls
- **Data Ownership**: Organizations maintain full data sovereignty

### Medical Device Disclaimer

The ECG Monitor system is not a medical device and is not FDA approved or CE marked. It is intended for monitoring and educational purposes. Healthcare providers must use clinical judgment and follow standard diagnostic protocols. Organizations are responsible for compliance with local regulations and obtaining proper informed consent.
