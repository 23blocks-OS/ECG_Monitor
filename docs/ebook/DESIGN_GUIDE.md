<a name="design-philosophy"></a>
## 1. Overall Design Philosophy

### Design Approach

**Emotional + Medical + Inspirational**

The design should balance three key elements:

1. **Emotional Impact**: This is a personal story that should feel intimate and authentic
2. **Medical Authority**: Include professional, clinical design elements that convey credibility
3. **Inspirational Energy**: Use dynamic imagery that conveys strength, recovery, and hope

### Visual Tone

- **Clean and modern** - not cluttered or overwhelming
- **Professional but approachable** - medical but not cold
- **Inspirational but not cheesy** - authentic emotion, not stock photo clichés
- **Urgent but not alarming** - convey importance without fear-mongering

<a name="layout-specifications"></a>
## 2. Layout Specifications

### Page Dimensions

**For Digital eBook (PDF/ePub):**
- Dimensions: 6" x 9" (standard book size)
- Or: 8.5" x 11" (US Letter) for easy printing
- Margins: 0.75" all sides (1" for print version)

**For Print-on-Demand:**
- Dimensions: 6" x 9"
- Margins: Inside 1", Outside 0.75", Top/Bottom 0.75"
- Bleed: 0.125" (if using full-bleed images)

### Layout Grid

- **Single column** for body text (easier to read)
- **Wide margins** for breathing room
- **Sidebar callouts** for key statistics or quotes in a different color/background

### Chapter Opening Pages

Each chapter should start on a new page with:

- **Chapter number** (small, top of page)
- **Chapter title** (large, bold, 1/3 down the page)
- **Opening quote or date** (if applicable)
- **Hero image or graphic** (full-bleed or 2/3 width)

### Body Pages

- **Header**: Chapter title (left) | Page number (right)
- **Footer**: Book title (left) | Author byline (right)
- **Line spacing**: 1.5x for readability
- **Paragraph spacing**: 0.5x line height between paragraphs

<a name="typography"></a>
## 3. Typography Guidelines

### Primary Font (Body Text)

**Option 1: Serif (Traditional, Professional)**
- Font: **Georgia, Garamond, or Merriweather**
- Size: 11-12pt
- Line height: 1.5
- Paragraph spacing: 0.5x line height
- Color: Dark gray (#333333) on white background (easier on eyes than pure black)

**Option 2: Sans-Serif (Modern, Clean)**
- Font: **Open Sans, Lato, or Source Sans Pro**
- Size: 11-12pt
- Line height: 1.5
- Color: #333333

**Recommendation**: Use **Serif for body text** (easier to read for long-form content) and **Sans-serif for headers and callouts**.

### Headers

- **Chapter Titles (H1)**: 32-36pt, Bold, Sans-serif (e.g., **Montserrat Bold** or **Roboto Bold**)
- **Section Headers (H2)**: 20-24pt, Bold
- **Subsection Headers (H3)**: 16-18pt, Semi-bold
- **Color**: Dark blue (#1a365d) or deep red (#991b1b) to convey urgency and medical themes

### Special Text Elements

- **Quotes/Pullouts**: 14pt, Italic, Serif, in a colored box with light background
- **Statistics/Numbers**: Large (24-30pt), Bold, Sans-serif, accent color
- **Captions**: 9-10pt, Light gray (#666666), Italic
- **Resources/Links**: 11pt, Medium weight, underlined or in accent color

### Drop Caps

For chapter opening paragraphs, use a **drop cap** (large first letter) to create visual interest.

## 5. Chapter-by-Chapter Image Suggestions

### General Image Guidelines

**Style**:
- High-resolution (300 DPI for print, 150 DPI minimum for digital)
- Authentic, not overly polished stock photos
- Mix of medical imagery, athletic imagery, and human emotion
- Use personal photos where possible (with permission)

**Treatment**:
- Option 1: Full-color with subtle overlay filter for consistency
- Option 2: Black & white with color accents (e.g., red heart, blue EKG line)
- Option 3: Duotone (red/blue) for dramatic effect

### Cover Image

**Concept**: Powerful, immediately recognizable, emotionally resonant

**Option 1: The Divided Heart**
- Left half: Athlete in motion (cycling or running), vibrant color
- Right half: EKG readout or medical imagery, clinical
- Center: A heart icon bridging both worlds
- Bold title overlaid

**Option 2: The Finish Line**
- Silhouette of athlete crossing Ironman finish line
- EKG line forming the finish tape
- Sunrise/dramatic lighting in background
- Title at top in bold letters

**Option 3: Before/After Split**
- Top: Athlete training (looking strong)
- Bottom: Same athlete in hospital bed (vulnerable but hopeful)
- Center: Date "August 13, 2024"

**Recommendation**: Option 2 (Finish Line) - most inspirational and forward-looking

### Title Page / Inner Cover

- Clean, simple
- Heart icon or EKG line graphic
- Author info
- "Free eBook - Share Freely to Save Lives" tagline

### Prologue: The Illusion of Invincibility

- Athlete in peak condition (triathlon bike, running, or at start line)

## 6. Design Elements & Graphics

### Recurring Visual Motifs

Use these consistently throughout the book for visual cohesion:

#### 1. EKG Line

- Use a **red EKG heartbeat line** as a decorative element
- Place at top or bottom of pages as a subtle border
- Use as dividers between sections
- Make it a key visual element on the cover

#### 2. Heart Icon

- Simple, modern heart icon (not cartoonish)
- Use in headers, bullets, or as accent
- Color: heart red (#DC2626)

#### 3. Timeline Graphics

- For chapters with chronological progression
- Horizontal line with markers/dates
- Use blue or red color

#### 4. Callout Boxes

**For Statistics**:
- Border: Thick red or blue bar on left side
- Background: Light gray (#F9FAFB)
- Large number in accent color
- Description text below

Example:
```
┃  50%
┃  of men who die suddenly from heart disease
┃  had NO previous symptoms
```

**For Key Takeaways**:
- Background: Light blue (#DBEAFE) or light green (#D1FAE5)
- Icon: Checkmark or lightbulb
- Bold header: "Key Takeaway"
- Body text in regular weight

**For Personal Quotes**:
- Italic text
- Quotation marks in large accent color
- Thin border or shaded background

#### 5. Icons

Use simple, line-style icons for:
- Checklist items (checkboxes)
- Resources (book icon, link icon, hospital icon)
- Prevention tips (shield icon, heart icon)
- Action steps (arrow icon, calendar icon)

**Icon Style**: Line icons (not solid), consistent weight (2px), one or two colors max

## 7. Production Formats

### Digital Formats

#### 1. **PDF (Reflowable or Fixed Layout)**

**Best for**: Desktop reading, printing at home, wide distribution

**Specifications**:
- Size: 8.5" x 11" (US Letter) or 6" x 9" (book size)
- Resolution: 150 DPI images (balance quality and file size)
- Fonts: Embedded (ensure fonts are included in PDF)
- File size target: Under 20 MB (for easy email sharing)
- Hyperlinks: Active links to resources
- Bookmarks: Chapter links in PDF navigation

**Tools**: Adobe InDesign (export to PDF), Canva, Google Docs (export to PDF)

#### 2. **ePub (Reflowable eBook)**

**Best for**: E-readers (Kindle, Kobo, Apple Books), mobile devices

**Specifications**:
- Reflowable text (adapts to screen size)
- Responsive images
- Accessible fonts (users can adjust size)
- Metadata: Title, author, description, keywords
- Cover image: 1600 x 2400 px (standard eBook cover)

**Tools**: Calibre (free), Vellum (Mac), Sigil (free, open-source)

#### 3. **Interactive PDF or Web Version**

**Best for**: Online reading, maximum engagement

**Features**:
- Embedded videos (if available - e.g., Ironman finish, patient testimonials)
- Interactive checklists (fillable forms)
- Clickable table of contents
- Embedded links to resources

**Tools**: Adobe InDesign (interactive PDF), Canva, or custom web page

### Print Format

#### Print-on-Demand (e.g., Amazon KDP, Lulu, Blurb)

**Specifications**:
- Size: 6" x 9" (standard paperback)
- Interior: Black & white or color (color is more expensive)
- Cover: Full-color, glossy or matte
- Binding: Perfect bound (paperback)
- Paper: White or cream (cream is easier on eyes)
- Bleed: 0.125" if using full-bleed images

**File format**: PDF/X-1a (for professional printing)

**Cover Design**:
- Front cover: High-impact image + bold title
- Spine: Title + author name (if thick enough)
- Back cover: Synopsis, key message, call-to-action, ISBN/barcode (if required)

<a name="accessibility"></a>
## 8. Accessibility Guidelines

To ensure the eBook is accessible to all readers:

### Text Accessibility

- **High contrast**: Dark text on light background (avoid light gray text)
- **Font size**: Minimum 11pt for body text
- **Line spacing**: 1.5x minimum (easier for dyslexic readers)
- **Font choice**: Avoid overly decorative fonts; use clean, readable fonts
- **Alt text**: For all images, include descriptive alt text (for screen readers)

### Structural Accessibility

- **Headings**: Use proper heading hierarchy (H1, H2, H3) for screen readers
- **Table of contents**: Include hyperlinked TOC for easy navigation
- **Lists**: Use proper list formatting (bullets, numbers)
- **Links**: Use descriptive link text (not "click here")

### Visual Accessibility

- **Color**: Don't rely on color alone to convey meaning (use text labels too)
- **Images**: Include captions or alt text
- **Graphs/charts**: Include text descriptions of data

### ePub/Digital Accessibility

- **Reflowable text**: Allow users to adjust font size
- **Semantic markup**: Proper HTML/ePub tagging for screen readers
- **Reading order**: Ensure logical reading order for assistive tech

- **Adobe Photoshop**: Professional editing
- **GIMP**: Free, open-source Photoshop alternative
- **Canva**: Built-in image editing and stock photos
- **Photopea**: Free, browser-based Photoshop alternative

### eBook Conversion

- **Calibre**: Free, converts between formats (PDF, ePub, MOBI)

## 10. Suggested Production Workflow

### Phase 1: Content Preparation (Complete)
✅ Manuscript written and edited

### Phase 2: Design & Layout (2-4 weeks)

**Week 1: Setup**
- Choose design software (Recommendation: **Canva for ease**, **InDesign for professional quality**)
- Select fonts and color palette (use recommendations above)
- Gather images (personal photos, stock images, medical illustrations)

**Week 2: Layout**
- Create master page templates (chapter opening, body pages, resource pages)
- Import text and apply typography styles
- Insert placeholder images

**Week 3: Visual Design**
- Design cover (front, back, spine if print)
- Create infographics (Know Your Numbers chart, timeline graphics, etc.)
- Insert final images with captions
- Design callout boxes and quotes

**Week 4: Finalization**
- Proofread (check for typos, formatting errors)
- Add hyperlinks (resources, TOC)
- Create table of contents with page numbers/links
- Export to PDF and ePub formats

### Phase 3: Review & Testing (1 week)

- Print a physical copy (even on home printer) to check layout
- Test ePub on multiple devices (phone, tablet, e-reader)
- Have 2-3 people review for clarity and impact
- Make final revisions

### Phase 4: Publication & Distribution (Ongoing)

- Upload to website for free download
- Share on social media
- Distribute via email
- Optional: Upload to Amazon KDP (free), Apple Books, Google Play Books for wider reach

## 11. Budget Estimates

### Free/Low-Cost Production

- **Design software**: Canva Free or Google Docs ($0)
- **Stock images**: Unsplash, Pexels ($0)
- **Icons**: Noun Project ($0 with attribution)
- **eBook conversion**: Calibre ($0)
- **Total**: $0-50 (if purchasing a few premium images)

### Mid-Range Production

- **Design software**: Canva Pro ($13/month) or Affinity Publisher ($70 one-time)
- **Stock images**: 10-15 images from Shutterstock (~$50-100)
- **Professional cover design**: Fiverr or 99designs ($50-200)
- **Total**: $150-400

### Professional Production

- **Design software**: Adobe InDesign ($20/month)
- **Stock images**: Adobe Stock or Shutterstock subscription ($30-50/month)
- **Professional book designer**: Freelance designer ($500-2000)
- **Medical illustrations**: Custom or BioRender ($200-500)
- **Total**: $1000-3000

**Recommendation**: Start with **Canva Pro** ($13/month) + free stock images. It's user-friendly, professional-looking, and affordable. You can create a high-quality eBook in 2-3 weeks.

## 12. Final Checklist

Before publishing, ensure:

- [ ] All chapters are complete and proofread
- [ ] Table of contents is accurate and hyperlinked (digital)
- [ ] All images are high-resolution (150 DPI minimum)
- [ ] All images have captions or alt text
- [ ] Cover is compelling and high-resolution (300 DPI for print)
- [ ] Fonts are embedded (PDF) or standard (ePub)
- [ ] Hyperlinks are active and correct
- [ ] Color palette is consistent throughout
- [ ] Infographics are clear and easy to read
- [ ] Resources section is complete with updated links
- [ ] Copyright/distribution notice is included
- [ ] Contact information or website link is included
- [ ] File size is reasonable for email/download (under 20 MB for PDF)
- [ ] Test on multiple devices (desktop, tablet, phone, e-reader)
- [ ] Print test copy to check physical appearance

## Quick Start: Canva Template Approach (Fastest Option)

If you want to create the eBook quickly using Canva:

1. Go to **Canva.com** (free or Pro)
2. Search for "**eBook template**" (6x9 or 8.5x11)
3. Choose a clean, modern template
4. Customize:
   - Replace template text with your manuscript
   - Change colors to heart red (#DC2626) and medical blue (#2563EB)
   - Upload personal photos or use Canva's stock images (search "triathlon," "hospital," "heart health")
   - Add EKG line graphics (search "heartbeat line" in Canva elements)
   - Create simple infographics using Canva's chart tools
5. Export as PDF (print quality)
6. Convert to ePub using Calibre (free)

**Timeline**: 1-2 weeks for a polished eBook

**Cost**: $0 (free Canva) or $13 (Canva Pro for one month)

This is the fastest, most accessible way to create a professional-looking eBook without design experience.
