Your Journey (When Available)

  1. Sign Up & Choose Plan

    Visit website, create account, select plan based on your needs (personal, small, medium, enterprise)

  2. Add Payment Method

    Secure payment processing via Stripe. Credit card, ACH, or invoice billing for enterprise.

  3. Devices Shipped

    Pre-configured devices shipped to your address (3-5 business days in US, international varies)

  4. Plug In & Auto-Connect

    Device automatically connects to your account on first power-on. No configuration needed.

  5. Create User Accounts

    Via web dashboard, create accounts for patients (or providers for practice plans)

  6. Start Monitoring

    Apply electrodes, turn on device, view real-time ECG data in dashboard immediately

Frequently Asked Questions

When will SaaS be available?

We're targeting Q2 2026 (April-June). Join the waitlist to be notified when we launch.

Can I switch from self-hosted to SaaS later?

Yes! We'll provide migration tools to transfer your data and devices to the managed platform.

Is there a free trial?

Yes, we plan to offer a 30-day free trial (you pay device cost upfront, refundable if you cancel).

Do I own the hardware?

Yes, the device is yours to keep. If you cancel subscription, device stops working but you keep it.

Where is data stored?

AWS US-East region by default. Enterprise customers can choose region or multi-region deployment.

Is this HIPAA compliant?

We're working toward HIPAA compliance for launch. Will require Business Associate Agreement for covered entities.

Can I export my data?

Yes, full data export available anytime in CSV or JSON format. You own your data.
