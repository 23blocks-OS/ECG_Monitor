⚠️ Important Medical Device Disclaimer

This is NOT an FDA-approved medical device. This is a research-grade monitoring system that should be used alongside, not instead of, standard clinical ECG equipment and protocols. Always follow established medical guidelines and use FDA-approved devices for clinical diagnosis.

Recommended Use: Supplementary monitoring, patient engagement, research, chronic disease management with appropriate clinical oversight.

What You Get

Clinical Features

  • 🤖 AI-Powered Analysis
    Claude 3.5 Sonnet detects arrhythmias: AFib, PVCs, PACs, bradycardia, tachycardia
  • 👨‍⚕️ Provider Dashboard
    Monitor multiple patients from single interface, search and filter, organization statistics
  • 👤 Patient Portal
    Patients view their own ECG data, health insights, and historical trends
  • 📧 Real-Time Alerts
    Email notifications when abnormalities detected (configurable thresholds)
  • 📊 Clinical Metrics
    Heart rate, HRV (RMSSD, SDNN), QRS width, R-R intervals, signal quality scoring
  • 🔒 HIPAA-Ready Infrastructure
    Encryption, access controls, audit logging foundation (add production auth)
  • 👥 Role-Based Access
    Admin, doctor, nurse, patient roles with appropriate permissions
  • 📱 Mobile App
    iOS/Android app for patients and providers (Expo-based)

Use Cases in Practice

Chronic Disease Management

Monitor patients with AFib, heart failure, or arrhythmias between office visits. Early detection of deterioration.

Post-Procedure Monitoring

Track patients after ablation, cardioversion, or cardiac procedures. Remote oversight during recovery.

Medication Titration

Monitor heart rate and rhythm while adjusting cardiac medications. Safer dose optimization.

Sports Cardiology

Athletes' cardiac monitoring during training and recovery. Detect overtraining or arrhythmias.

Patient Engagement

Empower patients to understand their cardiac health. Improve adherence and outcomes.

Research Studies

Collect ECG data for clinical research. Low-cost large-scale cardiac monitoring.

Integration with Clinical Workflow

  1. Patient Enrollment

    Identify candidates for remote monitoring. Obtain consent and explain the supplementary nature of the device.

  2. Device Distribution

    Provide Raspberry Pi ECG device to patient. Show how to apply electrodes and turn on device.

  3. Patient Portal Onboarding

    Create patient account, share portal URL, walk through dashboard features.

  4. Ongoing Monitoring

    Provider reviews dashboard periodically, receives email alerts for abnormalities.

  5. Clinical Decision Making

    Use ECG Monitor data to inform clinical decisions alongside standard diagnostic tools.

  6. Documentation

    Export relevant data for EHR documentation (manual process currently, API integration possible).

Deployment Timeline

Total Time: 2-3 hours

  1. Determine License Type (5 minutes)

    Are you nonprofit or for-profit? If for-profit, contact for commercial license.

  2. Deploy Infrastructure (30 minutes)

    git clone https://github.com/23blocks-OS/ECG_Monitor.git
cd ECG_Monitor
./install.sh

  3. Deploy Dashboards (30 minutes)

    Deploy provider and patient portals to Vercel or your own hosting.

  4. Create Organization & Users (30 minutes)

    Set up practice organization, add providers, create initial patient accounts.

  5. Configure Devices (30 minutes)

    Set up Raspberry Pi ECG monitors with certificates and configuration.

  6. Train Staff (30 minutes)

    Show providers how to use dashboard, interpret alerts, and manage patients.
